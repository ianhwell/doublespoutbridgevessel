Although the spotted cat in Nasca ceramics is a supernatural icon, it is rooted in the real world Andean feline including the Andean mountain cat or the jaguar. By using these feline characteristics and assigning them to anthropomorphic figures, the Nasca were probably showing themes of transformation. That transformation being where humans would adopt the physical and predatory traits of these super powerful elusive animals to then command some authority or spiritual protection.![Andean Mountain Cat](https://ianhwell.github.io/doublespoutbridgevessel/iiif/objects/juanreppucci-9andean-cat-2/juanreppucci-9andean-cat-2.jpg)
